What people usually ask us

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A landing page takes a few days; a custom app or automation usually takes 3 to 8 weeks. As a reference, I've taken a B2B platform from zero to production in one month. Your proposal includes a concrete, phased timeline.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted based on what you need. After our first conversation you get a fixed‑price proposal with no hidden costs. We can start with a first version and grow in phases.

What does it mean that you use AI to build?

We use AI tools to prototype, generate repetitive code and test faster. All code is reviewed and validated by a developer, so you gain speed without losing quality.

Do I own the code and the project?

Yes. At the end you receive the source code, the accounts and the documentation you need. Your project is 100% yours.

Can you automate a process we do by hand?

That's my specialty. If your team copies data between systems, builds reports in spreadsheets or repeats the same steps every day, there's probably an ETL pipeline, a bot or an AI agent that can do it. I've cut that kind of work by up to 85%.

Do you offer support after delivery?

Yes. Every project includes an initial support period and, if you need it, a maintenance plan with continuous improvements.

Do you work in English and Spanish?

Yes. I'm based in Orlando, FL and work remotely with clients across the US and Latin America, in English or Spanish.

Have an idea? Let's make it happen.

Tell us what you need and we'll reply within 24 hours with next steps. The first consultation is free.
